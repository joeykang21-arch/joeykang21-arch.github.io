---
title: "Zoom Out"
date: 2023-06-01
description: "A journal entry on embarrassing memories and how few people remember them."
category: personal
---

While my brain insists on replaying certain moments in my mind, I should revel in the fact that I am the only person on earth who can replay these scenes so vividly.

Think of every embarrassing, imperfect, cringe, or regretful moment of your life. Now zoom out... How many people in the history of time were witnesses? How many people still remember it? How many will remember it in 5 years? How about 10? 20? 100? 1000? 1 million? 1 billion? Why does this bother you then? Every second you spend dwelling on these scenes is one that can be spent on becoming more virtuous, useful, & peaceful. Remember the temporariness of it all. Do not let externals influence you & your character.
