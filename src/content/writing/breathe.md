---
title: "Breathe"
date: 2023-06-10
description: "A journal entry on giving each breath its own attention."
category: personal
---

The breath is the closest thing humans have to a magical power, and it is one of the few physical things we have almost complete control of. All the breaths that I have taken up until the last breath I ever take will be ubiquitous in that they will have come and went no matter my objections or desires.

However, I must try my best to give each unique breath its own time, its own attention, its own spotlight. Just like each moment in time, each breath is priceless and all we really have until the next breath passes us by.
