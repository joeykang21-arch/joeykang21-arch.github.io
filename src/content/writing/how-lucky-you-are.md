Dear Joseph,

Here is a quick reminder for you in case you have forgotten.

How lucky you are to be alive. The chances of being able to experience any of this are virtually zero. Just the advent of being able to feel any sort of consciousness is a gift of astronomical proportions.

How lucky you are to be born in the present time and place. You have better living conditions than virtually everyone who has ever lived.

How lucky you are to be born into such a loving family. Many people never get to experience the relationship of having such supportive and caring family members around them. The relationship you have with your parents and brother is something billions have spent their whole lives craving, and is more valuable than any material possession or physical pleasure you could ever hope to experience.

How lucky you are to be blessed with such a wide variety of talents. An absolutely miniscule number of people can pick different skills as easily as you do. Remember to appreciate all the gifts you have been given.

How lucky you are to have such great friends. Hundreds of millions of people live in loneliness, never being able to freely express themselves. It is a miracle you are able to get along with and thrive amidst so many different types of people.

How lucky you are to experience such joy at the activities you do. I have genuinely never seen anyone else with such incredible bliss when doing what they love. Think of all the incredible joy you derive at doing basic activities in life, and how miserable others seem in comparison when doing the same things that give you so much joy.

Never forget how absolutely phenomenal you have it.

All the best,

Joey
