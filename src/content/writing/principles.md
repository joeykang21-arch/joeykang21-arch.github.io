---
title: "Principles"
date: 2023-07-10
description: "A short list of principles to live by."
category: personal
---

1. Never turn your back on those you love
2. Virtue is the only good
3. Never wish to be someone else
4. Be content with nature's will
5. Stay as much in the present as you can and cherish every moment.
6. Live as though life can be taken away at any minute
7. Never stop being curious and learning
8. No one can be compared to anyone else
9. Never give up the awe, the wonder of what it means to ponder about the unknown
