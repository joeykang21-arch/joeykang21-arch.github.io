---
title: "The Sage"
date: 2023-06-18
description: "A journal entry on a moment of complete attention while meditating."
category: personal
---

Today, whilst meditating, I achieved a level of serenity and mindfulness I have rarely felt in my life. As I closed my eyes, I heard the sound of cars racing past far to my right. Then, I heard birds chirping far to my left. I felt the wind graze past my face. My butt and legs making contact with the turf. My hands and arms resting softly in my lap. The glow of the sun's rays laying on my skin. The bright ball of light casting its colors behind my eyelids. And then, I felt it all at once. The cars, birds, turf, sun, wind compelled my attention, and for a few moments, those things were all that ever existed.

The sage, I would imagine, would be able to sustain that level of serenity and mindfulness almost at will, regardless of his/her situation.
