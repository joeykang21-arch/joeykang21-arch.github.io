title: "Inner Citadel"
date: 2023-06-17
description: "A journal entry on self-worth and the approval of others."
category: personal
---

My quest towards inner peace and contentment has been tested over the past few days. I find myself to have my mind wandering and putting my self-worth in the hands of others.

Remember, the only things that can determine your self worth are yourself and Nature. The approval or praise of the masses has no effect on your character, on your moral excellence, on your inner citadel.

Remember that this will all pass soon. Live a life worth living while you have the opportunity. The present moment is truly a gift one cannot take away.

Remember that you are worthwhile, and no one can ever take that away from you.
