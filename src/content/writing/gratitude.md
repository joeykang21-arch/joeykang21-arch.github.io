title: "Gratitude"
date: 2023-01-29
description: "A journal entry on how unlikely it is to be here at all."
category: personal
---

When thinking about all the humans on earth, all the humans who have ever lived, all the organisms who have ever lived, and all the events that have transpired in this universe and all others to get to this moment cannot begin to be understood or articulated from a mind as feeble as my own.

Even if I were to cease to exist right after I finish writing this journal entry, the chances I would have gotten to experience any of the wonderful moments of my life are unfathomably small. Remember: all outcomes are fine.

Every moment of every day does not need to happen. You are not owed anything.

Everything that harmonizes with me is harmonious to thee, O Universe.
